# User Guide: AG_EPD Tag

## 1. Getting Started
1. Plug the Jogtek NFC Reader into your computer's USB port.
2. Launch `AG_EPD_Tag.exe`.
3. The application will automatically detect and connect to the active COM port.

---

## 2. Designing Your Tag Label
1. **Line 1 (Title)**: Type your site name, asset ID, or header text (e.g. `Site Name 1 -AAAA...`).
2. **Line 2 (Subtitle)**: Type your building name, location, or secondary description (e.g. `Building Name 1 -AAAAAA...`).
3. **Show Rounded Border**: Check or uncheck the box to enable or disable the perimeter frame.
4. **Live WYSIWYG Preview**: The preview box instantly reflects your changes. Long text will automatically scale to fit the screen.

---

## 3. Programming the Tag
1. Place a 2.13" EPD tag onto the reader antenna.
2. The status indicator will turn **Green (● Tag Ready to Program)** and display the tag's UID and firmware version.
3. Click the blue **"Program Tag"** button.
4. Keep the tag steady on the reader while the progress bar advances from $0\%$ to $100\%$.
5. When complete, an acoustic beep will sound, and the e-paper tag will refresh with your new text!
